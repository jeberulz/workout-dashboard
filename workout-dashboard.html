    Run it right

    • Warm up 5–10 min first — easy cardio + 2 light sets of your first lift.
    • Form before load. If reps break down, drop the weight, not the standard.
    • New to 6 days? Lots of viewers asked about recovery — start with Days 1–3 only (Push / Pull / Legs once) for 2–3 weeks, then add the B days.
    • Sleep & protein matter as much as the sessions on a twice-a-week split.
    • Sunday is off. Walk, stretch, eat — growth happens between sessions.
